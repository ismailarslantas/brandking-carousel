# Tool library

Over fifty signature tools and nineteen compositions. Each post uses **1-2 tools**, never all at once. Choose a composition, choose a tool, and name them. For which topic takes which tool, see [06-topic-matching.md](06-topic-matching.md).

Full-design images can use any of these. The engine's own layouts implement a smaller set; see [docs/PLAN.md](../docs/PLAN.md).

## Part 1: Signature tools

### A. Text carriers

1. **Frosted card:** translucent and blurred behind, radius 48, 1 px light border, 80 px from the edge. The most reliable tool.
2. **Highlight bar:** a separate rounded fill behind each headline line, with varied widths for a staircase effect.
3. **Color lower panel:** the bottom third is a solid brand panel with rounded top corners, holding the headline and CTA.
4. **Amorphous blob card:** an organic, asymmetric white form for Q&A and information text.
5. **Smoke or cloud transition:** photo fades into a white panel through smoke or cloud, not a hard edge.
6. **Chat bubble cards:** messaging-style bubbles for feature lists or problem and solution, aligned left and right.
7. **On-screen placement:** a laptop or phone screen becomes the frame, with the headline inside and cards overflowing.

### B. Directors and badges

8. **Diagonal arrow badge:** a rounded square (96 x 96, radius 28) holding a bold diagonal arrow in the accent color, overflowing a card corner.
9. **Pill CTA:** fully rounded and accent-filled, 88-104 px tall, arrow right and icon left.
10. **Double-layer price badge:** two nested pills, cream outside and brand color inside. The number is largest, the unit small.
11. **Number badge cards:** 2-3 rounded cards side by side, each with a giant number, a unit and a small label. For donation amounts, prices and packages.
12. **Segmented option strip:** one pill split by vertical lines (2+1 | 3+1 | 4+1), with a small label pill above.
13. **Label pill row:** audiences or categories side by side: "Schools / Teachers / Students / Parents". One filled, others translucent.
14. **Icon feature strip:** 3-4 icons side by side in rounded squares, two lines of text beneath.
15. **Icon bullet list:** an arrow or tick in a 44 px circle, in a vertical list. The backbone of information posts.
16. **Ticked pill list:** each item is a filled pill with a square tick box, stacked 3-5 high and slightly offset.
17. **Mini card stack:** three small cards on the left edge, each with an arrow badge and two lines, on a translucent dark field.
18. **App store badges:** in the footer strip for digital products.

### C. Card systems

19. **Bento grid:** 2 x 3 or 3 x 2 filled cards with a line icon and two lines each, equal sizes, 24 px gaps. One cell can hold a photo.
20. **Floating card constellation:** a device mockup at center with feature cards around it, softly shadowed and angled inward.
21. **Layered photo block:** two rounded photos offset over each other, with the headline and CTA beside them.
22. **Asymmetric corner card:** three rounded corners, one sharp. It adds direction, and is very strong in dark over a photo.
23. **Package cards:** three vertical cards, with the middle one taller and filled as the recommended tier. Name, giant number, unit and one line each, with a shared CTA below.
24. **Avatar row:** 4-5 round portraits overlapping 30%, with a thin white border, to show a team or community. Use only authorized portraits.

### D. Scene builders

25. **Metaphor prop:** the message becomes a real object: calendar (period), door hanger (invitation), signpost (coverage), key (handover), stopwatch (speed), flag (announcement), notepad (list). **This style's strongest move.**
26. **Magnifier:** a handheld lens with the subject sharp inside and blurred outside. For detail, inspection, quality.
27. **View through a frame:** an opening door, window or portal. The viewer stands at the threshold of the promised world.
28. **Out-of-bounds overflow:** an object breaks out of a frame such as a screen, card or circle, creating depth and attention.
29. **Venn circles:** two circles with the product in the overlap and one feature in each.
30. **Concentric rings:** tonal circles behind an object, locking it to the center.
31. **Arch mask:** a photo mask with a semicircular top. Classic and warm.
32. **Shaped photo mask:** the photo takes the brand symbol's form: house (property), chevron (progress), parallelogram (speed), drop (water), starburst (campaign), blob (organic). The mask holds the product entirely, with clean edges and no outline.
33. **Corner arc:** a brand-colored quarter circle or comma form in a corner, adding a turning motion.
34. **Frame within a frame:** a thin frame 40-60 px inside the canvas that gives a poster or notice feel.
35. **Natural framing:** plants, leaves or branches arc around the subject.
36. **Rim glow:** a light ring behind the object, making it a light source.
37. **Split-screen comparison:** a vertical or diagonal split with a black-and-white problem on the left, a color solution on the right and a thin brand divider.
38. **Text and object layering:** the headline passes behind the product, which covers part of the letters. Cover at most 30% and keep it readable.
39. **Reflection:** the product reflected in glasses, a mirror, water or a polished table, adding depth.
40. **Directing hands:** two hands point to, hold or create tension around the product, with shadows on the field.
41. **Drip or flow form:** liquid flowing down such as cheese, sauce, chocolate or paint, so the color block's lower edge is fluid. An appetite trigger for food.
42. **Duotone field:** the photo fully tinted to the brand color, with the prop or text white. Works with people too.
43. **Symbol field:** the message's symbol as a tone-on-tone silhouette: heart, drop, leaf or star.
44. **Organic blob field:** soft tone-on-tone waves that add motion to a flat field without blocking text.
45. **Category texture:** a sector surface: chalkboard (education), notebook lines (planning), kraft paper (handmade), circuit traces (technology), checkerboard (fast food, retro).
46. **Interface imitation:** generic like, comment and share icons, a chat screen, a search bar or a notification card inside the image. Strong for Q&A. **Use generic icons only, never a real platform logo, and never fake numbers or fake proof.**
47. **Color-coded variant:** each product variant on its own field with composition, type and angle fixed. The one exception to the variety rule.

### E. Conceptual moves

48. **Conceptual object arrangement:** objects form a figure: teeth into a clock, mint into a smiling face, dates into a question mark.
49. **Personification:** the product becomes a face or figure: toast and egg as a smile, with arms and eyes. Playful and shareable, strong in food and children's work.
50. **Surreal scale:** the product becomes something huge: a pastry slice as a mountain, a product as a hot air balloon. The scale shock draws attention.
51. **Giant type lock:** the product photo sits among giant letters and partly covers them. Capitals, tightly stacked.
52. **Repeat wall:** a repeated phrase becomes the texture, with the product or a hand in front. Either quiet tone-on-tone or high-contrast giant capitals.
53. **Diagonal type:** giant capitals on an angle in strips, sometimes in perspective over an object. Motion and energy.

### F. Presentation layer

For portfolios or client decks, place the design in a real setting: transit poster, billboard, phone, magazine, monitor. This is **a presentation format, not a post format**, and is not uploaded to Instagram this way. Deliver clean slides separately.

## Part 2: Compositions

| ID | Composition | Structure | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A** | Top headline | Headline at 120-480, centered or left. Hero at 500-1100. Footer below | Plain product intro, one message |
| **B** | Lower frosted card | Full-bleed photo, card at 780-1130, 80 px in. Arrow badge in a corner, CTA in the card | A strong scene photo. The most reliable |
| **C** | Upper color block | Top 0-430 solid brand. Left: headline. Right: dark card with arrow and supporting text. Photo below | Claim plus direction |
| **D** | Center hero | Object dead center, with ring, glow or natural frame behind. Two-line headline on top | One product, iconic launch |
| **E** | Conceptual arrangement | Flat or single-texture field. Objects form a figure. Short, playful headline | Interaction, memorability |
| **F** | Information card | Large central prop (paper, blob card, screen). Question headline on top, 4-6 icon points inside | Education, "what's in, what's out" |
| **G** | Prop scene | Flat brand field with a real object tilted at center. Headline top left, supporting right | Period, campaign, concept |
| **H** | Split comparison | Color block and headline on top. Lower two-thirds split into problem and solution, with chat bubbles | Objections, before and after |
| **I** | Lower feature strip | Top two-thirds photo, smoke transition to a white panel. Four icon features, headline below | Project intro, summary, grid close |
| **J** | Layered photo block | Two rounded photos offset, headline and CTA beside them | Interiors, portfolio, gallery |
| **K** | Bento grid | Two-line headline on top. 2 x 3 cards below with icon and two lines. One cell can be a photo | Feature lists, platform intro |
| **L** | Floating constellation | Device at center, 4-6 feature cards around it, headline on top | Software, apps, digital products |
| **M** | Number row | Headline on top, 2-3 number badge cards at center, photo or cutout below | Donations, pricing, statistics |
| **N** | Diagonal type | Flat or sky field. Giant capitals cross the canvas in angled strips. Product in a corner | Energy, campaigns, youth, food |
| **O** | Shaped mask | Flat or patterned brand field. Photo in a symbol-shaped mask at center. Corner arc | Property, construction, portfolios |
| **P** | Color-coded variant | Field in the variant's color with a tone-on-tone pattern. Giant product name, product at center. Fixed across the series | Menus, flavors, ranges, collections |
| **Q** | Package cards | Two-line headline, three vertical cards with the middle one taller and filled, shared CTA | Subscriptions, tiers, service levels |
| **R** | Interface imitation | Built like a generic screen: user row, question card, interaction icons | FAQ series, engagement, community |
| **S** | Hybrid poster | Hybrid type leads: tilted script on top, giant capitals below, a strip between. Product in the lower half on a checker or textured field | Fast food, cafes, retro, events |

Avoid decorative effects with no message, cliché office handshakes, invented platform screenshots, fake social proof, dense interface imitations, and masks that cut off a label.
