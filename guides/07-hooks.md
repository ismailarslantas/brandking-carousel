# Hook types

A hook is a promise made in eight words or fewer. Name the type in `meta.hook_type`, so `brandking ledger report` can compare types over time. Each type is a tool, not a formula, and each must be paid off inside the deck.

| Type | How it works | Example | Fails when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `command` | Tells the reader to do or stop something | Stop slicing it hot. | No reason follows |
| `warning` | Names a cost they are paying now | Your cover is hiding your best idea. | The cost is invented |
| `contrast` | Sets two things against each other | Heavy is not sturdy. | The contrast is fake |
| `question` | Asks what they already wonder | Why do readers stop at slide two? | Yes or no ends it |
| `number` | A count of something concrete | Three questions before you buy. | The list is padding |
| `specific_reader` | Names exactly who it is for | For bakers who sell out by ten. | The group is too wide |
| `before_after` | Shows a change and how it happened | From six ideas per slide to one. | It implies a result you cannot show |
| `myth` | Corrects a shared belief | Posting daily was never the fix. | Nobody holds that belief |
| `object` | Starts with one concrete thing | Turn the cup over first. | The object means nothing to them |
| `confession` | Admits a real mistake | We wasted a year on the wrong grid. | It did not happen |

## Choosing

1. Write five hooks across different types.
2. Cut any that could run on a competitor's account unchanged.
3. Cut any that promise more than the deck delivers.
4. Compare the rest with `brandking covers` at grid-tile size.
5. Choose the one you can state in a sentence, and say what it promises in `promise.text`.

## Slide two

Treat slide two as a second cover. Some readers meet a carousel there rather than on slide one, so it should make sense without what came before. Avoid opening with "This", "It" or "So".

## What not to do

Do not use false urgency, invented numbers, or a hook that withholds the point purely to force a swipe. A reader who feels tricked does not save the post.
